Bloom, without the secret handshake

Five takes on Bloom. All five share the new scrub: drag sideways on play and the bar grows (264×72 → 340×112) while play stretches into a wide chapter track with SponsorBlock marks and the chapter name and time on top. The difference is how each one shows that the four tools (quality, picture-in-picture, fullscreen, next) are in there: a visible key, peeking tools, tools always on the rim, tools that appear on pause, or a bar that shows you once. Quality opens its list right in the bar. Speed and audio only stay where they are, in the action row under the title. Hold-to-bloom and swipe-up still work on all of them.

The new scrub (all five)

  • Drag sideways on the play pill: −10 and +10 step aside, the bar grows to 340×112 and play stretches into a 316 dp chapter track — one segment per chapter, SponsorBlock marks along its bottom, a knob that stays under your finger (1:1, ≈2.4 s per dp on this 12:41 video).
  • The chapter name and time sit across the top while you drag; a tick on every chapter edge would be the haptic.
  • Two little grips on the play pill's ends hint that it slides.

Quality

  • Tapping Quality turns the bar into the list (320×340): Auto (with what it's playing now), 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p and a "Playing …" line, the transport still at the bottom. Rows rise in from the bottom.
  • Pick one: the check lands and the bar folds back to where you opened it from. Tap the page to back out.

Sizes

  • A Key — 322×72 at rest (adds a 52 dp ⋮ key).
  • B Crown — 264×72, four 30 dp tool nubs peeking over the top edge.
  • C Rim — 264×116, the four tools always on its top edge.
  • D Pause and E Peek — 264×80 with a chevron on the top edge.
  • Bloomed: 380×252 dome for all five.

Could be combined

These hints stack: e.g. B's crown with D's bloom-on-pause, or A's key with E's first-open peek.
