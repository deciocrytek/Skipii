Three themes that feel like Android

You asked for no glassy textures and to keep it Android. So this round has no glass, no glow, no blur, no textures and no special fonts. Every theme is built the way your Pixel builds its own.

How these are made

When you pick a wallpaper colour on a Pixel, Android grows a whole set of matching colours from that one colour, in a style you choose. These themes are made exactly that way, with Google’s own colour tool. So they sit next to your Pixel’s apps like they belong there.

colorsOne starting colourAndroid works out every shade from it: buttons, cards, the tab bar, light and dark. All of them are matched and readable.
tuneA Pixel styleYour Pixel offers the same styles under Wallpaper & style. Tonal spot is calm, with colour mostly on buttons. Vibrant is richer, and the colour reaches the cards and page. Monochrome has no colour at all.
interestsOne Android shapeEach theme has a signature shape from Android 16’s own shape set. It appears on small badges: the Settings icons and avatars with no picture. Skipii already uses the cookie shape for these.

The same in every theme: Skipii’s layout, its rounded font, its corners, the video, the black strip over the video, and SponsorBlock’s colours. No outlines and no tap ripples. Only the tab bar and the player bar float, on the plain shadow Android gives anything that floats.
