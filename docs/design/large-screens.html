Skipii on big screens: five directions

Skipii is a phone app today: portrait-locked, one column, and letterboxed on tablets and the Fold's inner screen. Here are five whole-app concepts for tablets and foldables, from the most standard Android layout (A) to the most unusual (E). Each concept shows Home on the unfolded Fold and on a landscape tablet, then Subscriptions, Shorts, Settings, watching a video, the fold ↔ unfold handoff, and (in A, C and D) the half-folded tabletop posture. Devices are drawn to scale in dp: Fold inner 700×840, Fold cover 373×904, tablet 1280×800. Video titles and channels are made up for the mockup.
