Brightness and volume you can see

GitHub #88: in fullscreen the level pill sits right under the thumb that moves it, and the two pills sit at different distances from their edges. The reporter's examples are sideways bars and rings; here a swipe up always fills upward. Every phone below is live: drag up or down on its left third (brightness) or right third (volume).

Every option also fixes the uneven edges: today the brightness pill sits about twice as far in as the volume pill, because only the left edge has the camera and only that side was padded for it. Across pads both sides by the camera's edge; the other three don't touch the edges at all. Made by Claude and Neon for GitHub #88. Phones are about 915 × 412 dp; the thumb is drawn to show what it hides (turn it off with Show thumb).
