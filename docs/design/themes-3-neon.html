Round 3 · Rethinking Skipii

Let the video own the colour. Let the theme set the room.

The design idea

Skipii is a video player first. Every thumbnail is already loud with faces, bright colours, and big text—so the app around it should act like a quiet frame, never a second billboard.

Round 1 only swapped paint colours. Round 2 added textures and strong glows that competed with the videos. A better Skipii theme stays completely smooth and texture-free, and uses just four dials beyond colour:

  • Corner shape Shifting from soft pebbles to crisp cinema frames changes the whole mood without adding clutter.
  • Heading font Swapping the title voice changes how the app feels while leaving thumbnails untouched.
  • Surface depth Choosing flat tonal layers versus gentle floating bars sets the sense of calm.
  • One quiet glow A soft coloured shadow on just one or two hero controls (like Play and the active tab), never animated, never loud.
Canopy Medium · 1–2 days

Forest emerald with extra-soft pebble corners, friendly humanist titles, gentle bar lift, and a quiet jade glow on Play + active tab.

Dials: Extra-round shape · Plus Jakarta Sans · Soft lift · 2 glows
Folio Medium · 1–2 days

Deep fig-mulberry on warm cream or charcoal, crisp camera-frame corners, magazine serif titles, completely flat bars, and glow on Play only.

Dials: Crisp 10dp shape · Fraunces serif · Flat print tone · 1 glow
Zephyr Light · 2–3 hours

Airy sky-cerulean on clean snow or graphite. Keeps Skipii's existing corner sizes so it ships fast, with Outfit titles, lifted capsules, and 2 crisp sky glows.

Dials: Native shape · Outfit geometric · Capsule lift · 2 glows
