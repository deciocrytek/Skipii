Double tap to like, ten ways

Each phone plays a double tap, then two more quick taps. The circle is where the finger touched. Click twice on any phone to try it yourself. Every option starts above the finger, so the fingertip never hides it. The bar's like button fills on the first like.

What they share

Every option likes on the second tap, never unlikes, and a single tap still pauses after the 300 ms double-tap wait. Each further quick tap adds another one. They are drawn over the picture (only G moves it, a short jolt), and with animations off they just fade in and out.

YouTube today

Since June 2026 YouTube has been swapping the Shorts thumbs-up for a heart (like button and double tap), first as a test, then more widely. These keep Skipii's thumb. Any of them can take a heart instead if you'd rather match the new look.
