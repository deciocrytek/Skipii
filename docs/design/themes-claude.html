Lagoon, Marquee and Ink

Three new themes for the Appearance page, each written out by hand for light and dark like Jai and Pumpkin, and checked with AMOLED on. Every phone below is drawn only from the theme's own colour roles, placed the way the app uses them: cards, the tab bar, the player bar, Settings and the theme picker.

Switch each theme between Light, Dark and AMOLED with its buttons. Neon's three are in a separate page.

How they'd ship

Each one becomes a written-out scheme in the app's theme file and a new swatch in Appearance, tagged New. Nothing else in the app changes: the video, the black status strip over it, SponsorBlock's colours and the shorts glass stay as they are.

The app copies the secondary container into the tertiary container for every theme, so the "New" tags in the picker use that tone.
