Android-native themes for Skipii

“Android-native” means Skipii looks and feels like a built-in Pixel app: solid, calm Material 3 Expressive tones, Pixel’s own rounded lettering, and zero glass, blur, grain, or glow. Cards and bars stand apart by gentle steps in lightness alone.

Each theme below changes four things: the seed colour it starts from, the Material colour style that decides how colourful the background and cards get, the highlight colours on buttons and tabs, and one optional Material shape for letter avatars and Settings icons. Try Light, Dark, and pure-black AMOLED on each one to see how it holds up.
