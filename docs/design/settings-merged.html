Skipii Settings: A + C, four ways

From A (Ledgii): the account card, tiles that summarise what's set, Theme inline on the front page, Ledgii's header and motion. From C: a search field that finds any setting, labelled groups, rows that join into one block. Each example has three phones: the front page, a search in progress, and a category. Type in any search field. Tapping a result opens its category and highlights the setting.

2Connected tiles

Ledgii's tiles keep their shape (icon square, summary, chevron) but join C-style inside each group: 3 dp apart, small inner corners and 21 dp outer corners, so the page reads as four blocks instead of nine cards. Theme becomes the first row of "Look & language" and opens in place. Category pages join their rows the same way, under Ledgii's UPPERCASE labels.

3Search bar on top

The title row becomes a search bar, like Google's own apps, with your avatar at its right end. The avatar replaces the big account card and opens Account. Below it: Ledgii's Theme card, then connected groups with C's sentence-case labels.

4Big title that folds

C's large title and search at the top. Scroll, and they fold into Ledgii's header, with search turning into an icon there (scroll the first phone). Instead of the Theme card, the most-used things sit up front as four quick toggles, like Quick Settings. Categories fold the same way; the third phone is already scrolled.

Trade-off

The most motion, and it matches the collapsing headers on Skipii's other tabs. You'd choose which four toggles go up front; I guessed Audio only, SponsorBlock, Shorts and Keep speed.

My lean: 2, Connected tiles. It keeps the parts of Ledgii you see most (summary tiles, account card, labels, Theme up front), and the joined groups plus search keep the page short as categories pile up. 4's quick toggles and folding title could go on top of it later.
