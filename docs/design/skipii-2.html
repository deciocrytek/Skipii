Design proposals · 23 November 2026

Skipii 2.0

Skipii 0.5 already does the everyday things well. 2.0 is about the moments it still can't cover: no signal, finding things, a feed you can trust, falling asleep to a video, and a player bar that has run out of room. Claude and Neon (Gemini) argued it out over two rounds; these are the eight ideas both of us would build, in the order we'd build them.

Where we agreed

  • NeonDownloads come first. They're the last unfinished item on the original plan, and Skipii already keeps Shorts on the phone the same way.
  • ClaudeSearch is the weakest screen. I checked: it shows one page of videos and stops. No channels, no playlists, no suggestions.
  • BothThe player bar's tool row is full. At 7 small circles with no labels it's already cramped, and Sleep and Loop would make 9.

Where we disagreed

  • ClaudeNeon wanted a "Now playing" picture card in audio only. Dropped: you chose no picture there on purpose (0.5.36).
  • NeonFour tabs plus a separate search button breaks your five-tab rule. Shown as an option (section 3), not a recommendation.
  • ClaudeI added cleaner titles, feed filters, a home-screen widget and one settings button in fullscreen. Neon backed all four, and talked me out of a glow behind the video, which would fight the pure-black look.

Dropped
No more bot check, age-restricted videos

Needs YouTube's own player code to run inside Skipii. Still blocked; downloads soften it in the meantime.

Blocked

Behind-the-scenes tidy-ups (Neon's list)

One picker per control

Speed, quality and captions each exist in five versions (bar, sheet, fullscreen card, tabletop, TV). Sharing one makes every new control, like Sleep, a single job.

Back that remembers

Channel → another channel → Back currently can't return to the first. A proper page history fixes that and keeps slide animations consistent.

Fewer repeated settings

"Keep speed" lives in three places. The quick toggles on the Settings front page could hold Downloads or Background play instead.

Lighter comments

Comment names are looked up one channel feed at a time, through feeds that often fail. Plain @handles (as YouTube now shows) would load faster and use less data.

Mockups use made-up channels and videos. Colours follow Skipii's YouTube theme in light and dark.
