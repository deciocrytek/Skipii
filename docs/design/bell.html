The bell, on the Subscribe button

Bell on = a phone notification for every new video from that channel (YouTube's “All”). Bell off = no notifications from Skipii. It reads and writes the same setting as YouTube, so turning it on here turns it on in the YouTube app too. Both phones in each option are live: tap the buttons.

ASplit · a bell key beside Subscribed

Once you're subscribed, a small bell key grows out of the button's right side, joined to it. One tap on the bell turns notifications on: the key fills with the theme colour, goes round and rings. Tap it again to turn them off. Subscribed still unsubscribes in one tap, exactly like today.

BMenu · the bell lives inside Subscribed

Subscribed carries the bell as its icon: outline when it's off, and with the bell on the whole button turns the theme colour. Tapping Subscribed opens a small card under it: an “Every new video” switch and Unsubscribe. Two taps to change the bell, but you can't unsubscribe by accident.

Turning it off

Sets YouTube's Personalized (YouTube's default), so the YouTube app goes back to how it was. Channels on Personalized or None both show the bell off.

First time

The first bell turned on asks Android's “Allow Skipii to send notifications?”. Said no = the bell stays off with a note pointing to Settings.

Shorts

The Shorts channel card (Subscribe · Go to channel) gets the same bell: A as a key on its Subscribe row, B as an “Every new video” switch row.
