Morph stack, in full

GitHub #88, Claude's detailed version. Ten dots stand a third of the way in from the side you swipe. The dot at your level grows into a capsule as your finger moves and pops when it fills; the number rides beside it and rolls upward; a solid badge sits underneath. Everything that follows your finger is redrawn every frame, so it never lags. Drag the phone's left third for brightness, the right third for volume, or watch the demo.

How it moves

Two kinds of motion. What follows your finger is drawn straight from the finger, every frame. The flourishes (pops, rolls, bounces) are springs layered on top that never hold the shapes back.

MomentWhat you seeTiming
Finger downThe badge pops up first, then the dots rise into place from the bottom, one after another.Badge 240 ms overshoot; dots 18 ms apart, 200 ms each
DraggingThe dot at your level grows into a capsule in step with your finger. 40% of the screen's height is the whole range.Every frame, no delay
A slot fillsIt pops a size bigger and settles.Spring, bouncy (damping 0.45, stiffness 700)
A slot emptiesA small shrink, then back to a dot.Same spring, half the kick
Top capsuleThe bigger "top" size slides from one slot to the next as the next one grows. Nothing jumps.Follows the finger
NumberRides beside the top capsule, on the side facing the middle of the screen (never over the empty dots); its digits roll up when the value rises and down when it falls.Spring, very stiff (about 60 ms)
Reaching 100%The number pops and the top capsule gives an extra-big pop. Nothing flies off it.Same springs, double kick
Pulled past the topThe slots spread apart like an accordion, more slowly the further you pull.Up to 35% taller
Reaching 0%Volume: the badge turns grey, the speaker gets a slash, it shakes its head. Brightness: the sun dims.Shake spring, about 400 ms
Pulled past the bottomThe slots squeeze together and the badge squashes.Up to 35% shorter

Corner cases

Your thumb

A thumb works in the outer sixth of the screen. The stack's centre line is about 200 dp further in, so nothing of it is ever under the thumb.

Even edges

The lanes are measured from the screen, not the camera, so brightness and volume mirror exactly whichever way the phone is turned.

Short windows

Split screen or a short pane: slots move closer (down to 12 apart) and the badge shrinks to 36, so the group always fits between the top and bottom 28 dp.

Captions on

The group sits between about 20% and 80% of the height; captions sit lower and are never moved or covered.

Auto-brightness

The first touch starts from where the system's slider is. Dragging sets this window only; leaving fullscreen hands brightness back to the system.

Volume buttons

Pressing the phone's volume buttons in fullscreen could show the same stack on the volume side, so both ways look alike (optional).

Swipes turned off

If brightness or volume swipes are off in Settings, that side never shows a stack.

Bright and dark films

No box: every shape and the number carry their own shadow, so they read on a white sky and on black. Try "Dark scene".
