Every playback control, inside the bar

Five ways to move everything off the video and into the player's bar (0.5.32's 264×72 "Lift" bar is the starting point): play, ±10, the scrubber with chapters and SponsorBlock marks, time, speed, audio only, quality, picture-in-picture, fullscreen, next, and the SponsorBlock Skip prompt. The video itself stays clean: a single tap does nothing, double-tap seek still works. Every phone is live — tap, swipe, drag and hold on the bars; the demos show each one's moves.

Shared across all five

  • SponsorBlock Ask grows a row into the top of the bar ("Skip self-promo" · ✕); "Skipped sponsor · Undo" would use the same row.
  • The play pill stays the progress bar (pale track, full-primary fill). Speed and quality cycle on tap here; the full ruler and quality sheet stay one long-press away (D shows them in the bar itself).
  • Picture-in-picture, fullscreen and next show a little confirmation above the bar in the mockup — in the app they'd just happen.

What this would overturn

  • CLAUDE.md's "player controls overlay the video" and "chapters live on the video (label + scrubber segments)". Picking one of these replaces both: chapters move to the bar's scrubber.
  • Fullscreen/landscape still needs controls on the video (the bar isn't there) — the overlay would stay for fullscreen only.
