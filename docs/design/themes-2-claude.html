Glasshouse, Afterglow and Arcade

This round each theme is a material, not only a set of colours. Glasshouse is frosted glass in dappled daylight. Afterglow lets the video light the room around it, through a fine film grain. Arcade is neon tubes over a glowing floor grid.

Every effect stays off the video picture itself and behind the words. Each theme has light, dark and AMOLED versions. Under each one: what every effect is made of, what it costs the phone, and what happens when Android's reduce-motion setting is on.

One setting for all three

Appearance would gain a Glow & texture row: Full, Soft (half strength) or Off. Off leaves just the colours, so each theme still looks finished as a plain colour theme. Reduce motion turns every pulse into a steady glow.

What stays the same

The video, the black status strip over it, SponsorBlock's colours and the Shorts glass are untouched. Nothing is drawn with a thin outline. Surfaces are told apart by tone, light and shadow.
