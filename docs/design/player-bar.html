The player's own bar

While a video is open, the tab bar doesn't leave — it turns into the player's bar, same capsule, same spot, with play and skim-ahead buttons in reach of your thumb. Minimize and it turns back into tabs. Tap the mini-player to open, drag the video down (slowly, too) to watch the bar morph back under your finger.

A · Same pill, new job Four tabs become four controls. The red selected-tab pill slides one slot over and becomes Play, with the time in it. Taps on ±10 add up like double-tap seek; ⏭ jumps to the next chapter.
B · The bar is the scrubber The pill folds into a play button, then unrolls with the video's progress filling it. Slide along it to skim — a preview frame rides above your finger. +10 on the right.
C · Split dock, hold to skim The capsule pulls apart like a drop: play on its own, controls beside it. Tap Skim for +10 s; hold it to watch at 2×, slide right for 3× and 4×, let go for 1×.

All three

  • Same 62 dp capsule, 328 dp wide, same place as the tab bar. No new chrome: the tab bar itself changes job.
  • The morph is driven by the same progress as the mini ↔ player morph, so a swipe down scrubs it 1:1 and a release settles it with the page.
  • The overlay on the video stays as it is (and is still what fullscreen uses). The bar is the always-there, thumb-height copy — handy now that controls start hidden.
  • Steps aside (slides down + fades) for fullscreen, PiP, the comments panel and sheets. Works the same in Audio only.

A · Same pill, new job

Closest to what's there. One-to-one slot mapping: tabs drop out left to right, the selected-tab pill glides (with a little stretch) to slot 2, then the transport icons spring up from below, centre first.

⏭ = next chapter (next in queue when the video has no chapters). ±10 bursts show a rolling total above the button — the same language as double-tap seek.

B · The bar is the scrubber

Most "skim". The capsule pinches into a 62 dp circle with Play inside, then unrolls; the progress fill sweeps in after it. Chapter starts are small dots along its bottom edge.

Dragging anywhere in the middle maps the whole bar to the whole video and shows a preview frame + chapter above the finger; letting go seeks there (with the usual audio fade).

C · Split dock, hold to skim

Most playful. A liquid split (metaball) separates Play into its own circle; the rest spring in from behind it. Skim: tap = +10 s, hold = 2× while held, slide right for 3× / 4× (a step haptic each), release = back to your speed.

Hold never changes the saved speed setting; it's momentary, like YouTube's press-and-hold.

How it would be built

NavPill gets a second content slot, PlayerBar, and both read morph.progress in graphicsLayer only (no recomposition per frame). Springs set visibilityThreshold 0.0005 like the morph. No ripple, presses are pressable; the tab bar's hit target is handed to the bar at progress 0.5.
