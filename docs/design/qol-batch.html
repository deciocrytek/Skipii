1 · Screen lock in fullscreen #63 · #55

A lock tile joins the overlay's top row. Locked, the video takes no touches: no seeking, swipes, brightness/volume or pinch. A tap shows only the lock pill. Holding the pill for 1 s fills its ring and unlocks. Back also unlocks. The lock lasts until you leave fullscreen.

Play all and Shuffle sit under the playlist title and queue the whole list ([current, ...queue]). The long-press sheet also gets "Remove from Watch later" / "Remove from <playlist>". Both use the existing setInPlaylist(add = false).

4 · "Subscriptions" fits the tab bar #39 · #70

5 · Background play switch #68

Settings › Playback gets a "Background play" switch, on by default. When it's off, leaving the app or turning the screen off pauses the video, unless picture-in-picture or audio only is on (audio only is the "I want sound in my pocket" mode). It takes effect immediately.
