Three frames for the videos

You said the last round went too far. This round has no textures at all. Glow is held back to the one or two things you’re about to press, and it stays still. Before the themes, here is what I think a Skipii theme should be.

The idea

The videos are the colour. Skipii is the frame around them. A good theme changes the frame’s character (its shape, its type, how it sits) and keeps it quiet enough that every thumbnail still looks like the brightest thing on the screen.

paletteColourA page tone and one accent. The accent appears only where you act: play, the selected tab, chosen filters, what’s switched on, progress.
rounded_cornerShapeHow round the corners are, from crisp to soft. It is one set of five sizes for each theme, and the whole app follows it.
match_caseTypeThe font for headings and titles only. Small text and numbers stay in the plain app font so they’re always easy to read.
layersDepthEither flat, where surfaces are told apart by tone, or lifted, where cards sit on soft shadows. Never outlines.
wb_incandescentGlowOff, or a soft still halo on at most two things: the play button and the selected tab. It never pulses and never touches the video.

The same in every theme: the layout, the video itself, the black strip over the video, SponsorBlock’s colours, the Shorts glass, and the rules (no outlines, no tap ripples, text always readable).

What building any of these needs, once

Today a theme in Skipii can only change colours. Lagoon was quick to build for that reason. To let a theme change shape, type and depth, the app needs three switches built in once. After that, each new theme is a short list of numbers, as quick as Lagoon was.

  1. Corner sizes become part of the theme. About 85 places in the app write their corner size directly. They would read one of five sizes from the theme instead. This is a careful job of medium size. Existing themes keep exactly today’s sizes, so they don’t change.
  2. The heading font becomes part of the theme. Headings name the rounded font directly today. They would ask the theme instead, and the new fonts ship inside the app, adding about 0.3 MB each.
  3. Shadows and glow become part of the theme for cards, the tab bar, the play button and the selected tab. It’s the same kind of shadow the brightness and volume stack already draws, and a still glow costs nothing while you watch.

Then any of the three themes is a small job. Streetlight could ship first, because it keeps today’s shapes and font and only needs the glow.
